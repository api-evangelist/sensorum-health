# Sensorum Health

> Sensorum Health provides home-based, fully passive sensor technology and integrated clinical services that monitor older adults with chronic conditions 24/7 — with no wearables, smartphones, or WiFi required — to identify and triage the earliest signs of health decline and prevent avoidable hospital admissions. Backed by GV (Google Ventures). No public API or developer program is currently published.
